# s510 gas flow instability
Sensor s510 measures a process gas mass-flow reading. Failing lots run higher (about 74 vs 54). A noisy or drifting mass flow controller (MFC) causes non-uniform films. Check: plot s510 over time and look for step changes; a step that lines up with a fail-rate jump points to an MFC swap or calibration. Action: recalibrate or replace the MFC.
