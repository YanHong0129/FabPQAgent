# s519 etch rate excursion
Sensor s519 is a proxy for plasma etch rate. High s519 (fail mean about 12.4 vs pass about 8.8) means over-etch, which thins critical layers and shows up as parametric failures. Common causes: chamber wall condition after a wet clean, RF power drift. Check whether high-s519 lots cluster right after a chamber clean. Action: run seasoning wafers and re-qualify etch rate.
